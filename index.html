01 About

I have always been drawn to problems that require both intuition and careful reasoning.

My background in mathematics and physics has shaped the way I approach them: I try to begin from first principles, develop an intuitive picture of the underlying mechanism, and then test that understanding through precise analysis. I value clarity over unnecessary complexity and enjoy learning whatever a problem demands.

Machine learning appeals to me because it brings theory, implementation, and experimentation into a single process. I find it especially exciting that we can design systems that learn meaningful structure from data, translate mathematical ideas into working models, and refine them through empirical evidence. This interplay between abstract reasoning and practical results is what motivates me to study machine learning and pursue research in the field.

03 Research

Selected research

Work at the intersection of machine learning, medicine, and efficient computation.

01
Biomedical AIVision–Language ModelsExplainability

ConceptCLIP-V2

Advisor: Prof. Hao Chen

An explainable biomedical vision–language pre-training framework designed to learn human-interpretable concepts while accounting for the distinct characteristics of different medical imaging modalities. The project explores how concept-level learning and modality-specific specialization can make biomedical models more transparent and effective across diverse forms of medical data.

02
Efficient MLAttentionScale Robustness

Efficient Attention under Local Scale Variations

Advisor: Dr. Mohammad Mahdi Mojahedian

An efficient attention mechanism designed to remain robust when individual objects within an image undergo independent changes in scale. The project investigates how local scale robustness can be incorporated directly into attention while avoiding the computational cost of conventional multi-scale processing.
